Privacy, in plain words

Your full video is never uploaded. Your browser extracts about 16 still frames and a compressed copy of the audio track. Only those are sent for analysis.

Analysis is done by Google Gemini. The frames and audio are sent to Google's Gemini API. On the free tier Google may use this content to improve their services. Do not upload reels containing information you can't share. See Google's Gemini API terms.

What we store. We keep the analysis results (scores, detected features such as "hook starts at 2 seconds", the written report, your niche, video length, and the optional numbers you enter like followers, usual views and caption). We do not store the frames, the audio or the video. We store a hashed, non-reversible version of your IP address for 24 hours only to enforce the daily free limit.

Results you report. If you later enter the real views for a reel, we store them together with the prediction. This data is used to measure and improve the accuracy of predictions.

On your device. Your recent reports are saved in your browser's local storage so you can reopen them. Clearing site data removes them.

Contact. Questions or deletion requests: replace this line with your email.
